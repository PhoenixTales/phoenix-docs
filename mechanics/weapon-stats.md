# Weapon Stats

*flosha*, 11.09.2026  
*Heretyk & SaluteAdEzio & Avallach*  

According to the Alpha we follow the following structure: 
* Old/rusty weapons have a damage between 1-5.
* "Medium/regular" weapons have a damage between 6-10.
* Heavy/High grade weapons have a damage between 11-15. 
  * Melee weapons have a maximum damage of 15.

SaluteAdEzio:  
* Daggers are good for exploiting gaps in armour and once you get in range you have an advantage against an enemy with a longer, less maneuverable weapon. To represent that -> Daggers have higher damage compared to other weapons with the same requirements. 
* Daggers are best to quickly deal with an unsuspecting enemy (stealth kill mechanic aside, as you can quicky spam high dmg attacks). On the other hand daggers are bad against [most] monsters or groups of enemies.

Damage types:
* Stabs - highest lethality (internal organs), ok against armour (exploit gaps), low stopping power (a wound can be mortal but not incapacitating) [bleed mechanic would be best but I don't know how would that fit]
* Cuts - high stopping power, but generally less lethal (incapacitating but survivable), worst against armour 
* Blunt - good against armor, ok stopping power and lethality

How I would do it gameplay wise:

| Piercing Weapons   | Cutting Weapons | Blunt Weapons |
|--------------------|-----------------|---------------|
| [+] Highest Damage | [+] Best Reach  | [+] Most armors have low blunt resistance |
| [=] most armors have medium piercing resistance | [=] normal damage | [=] medium reach (they are heavy) |
| [-] restricted to daggers -> short reach | [-] most armors have high cut resistance | [-] low damage / slow |

Avallach's idea: Light but sharp weapons like daggers or rapiers have low base damage but higher critical hit chance. And a critical hit, representing piercing a gap in armor, bypasses even very high damage negation. While heavier and blunter weapons have low critical hit chance and as such may be entirely or almost entirely negated by a sufficiently strong armor but can reliably take down unarmored enemies with a few hits. 

SaluteAdEzio: 
* No armor/light armor opponents - animals, diggers, shadows/bandits - have low cutting resistance, so swords are fine against them
* Heavy armored opponents - guards, merceneries, templars, minecrawlers - it's a good idea to pick a mace, as sword will have issues
* Daggers are a high risk, high reward option
