# Character Sheet

*flosha, 08.08.2026*

Info: A rough WIP mockup of the character sheet; first in text form before applying any design.

General guidelines: We use the latest known top layout as presented in v0.94k, but we do not display the spells on the screen, because we will include a spell book for this purpose or a category in the log/diary. We also do not display active missions in the character screen, because they will be presented in the log/diary as well. Instead the character sheet will present the following information about the NPC and in this order:

1. Name/Nickname (in case of the player character it is the nickname that is given to him by NPCs in the game world, the one that the other characters adapt). 
2. Guild (in earlier documents also called "Bande")
3. Rank within his guild (which roughly corresponds to the Level (there will be 6 or 7 total levels), while the Level roughly corresponds to the Chapter he is in, which is one of six). 
4. Primary Attributes in descriptive clear text form
   4b. Secondary/Status Attributes (maybe displayed in icon form as within the Icon HUD, maybe in form of numbers, or maybe they will not be displayed in the Character Sheet at all, as the HUD or the Visualisation is meant to suffice: When using the Icon HUD you know that you are hungry and how much, you know how much Mana you have or not and how much you can currently have; you know all this info already and don't need it again in the character sheet. In case of the Bar HUD we try to provide the same information in an unobtrusive way and to also enable gameplay without any HUD we try to provide all this information immersively as well (via animations, via verbal cues, sound effects and pfx effects). 
5. Skills/Talents (maybe only those skills/talents that the character, based on his guild or class, is able to learn; that means in the beginning only very basic skills
