# Roadmap

*Flosha, 02.08.2026*


## What has been achieved as of 2026

As of now, Phoenix is in development since about 10 years. In these years a lot has been achieved, mainly the Gothic Archive with several phases of new publications and digitisation of material that hasn't been seen, our Alpha Research based on our Archive, in which we could clarify many questions that people have been speculated about for many years, and our Phoenix Documentation (partially public), in which we have developed our vision (both the game's mechanics and story) to a highly detailed degree.

Substantial progress also has been made in regard to our world model. Years have been spend with the reconstruction of earlier versions of the world model based on screenshots, which later turned out to be unnecessary (or to be mere modeling practice), as we received the original models of these very versions in course of the years. Since then much work has been invested in the reconstruction of world concept art and written ideas about the world, that haven't been modeled ever. 

We also have made much progress in regard to 2D art, where we have reconstructed (and in many cases improved) all of the armours and clothes of older versions. 


## Current Problems

But indeed we also have struggled in many regards. Phoenix is a non-commercial project. Everything is done in our free time, by a little group of enthusiastic people. Most of us have little time, sometimes with weeks or months of inactivity due to our personal life and work taking priority. And the ambition of our project is much higher tham the perceived interest of others in this alpha vision that inspires us so much, due to which we haven't as much active help and support as we would need to make more substantial and faster progress.  

On the programming front years have been spend by Auronen, not on actually implementing any features for Phoenix, but on fixing existing problems with the game and the engine that persist to this day. Many things have been achieved in this regard too, and Auronen could probably write long articles only about this kind of work that has been going on in the background. 

Most of this very important and necessary work doesn't affect the gameplay mechanics of Phoenix directly, but it is the necessary basis on which we can realise these mechanics. Therefore it doesn't seem to offer much to the player and its value cannot immediately be seen, but it is nonetheless necessary. 

Some people may also be inclined to accuse us of feature creep. The project was, originally, indeed intended to be much smaller in scope. But so was our Gothic Archive and what we knew about the original vision. As our understanding of the early vision has grown, so our concept and planning has grown. Because the ultimate goal of our project, as written so often, was never to reconstruct a specific alpha version or version of the story, but one coherent whole. The main goal was to dig deep into this world and get everything out of it. We want to fulfill it in all of its potential. And since that potential is much more than what we originally imagined, we had to overwrite our concept to do justice to this potential.  
As of now, this concept is set. We digged deep enough and all that is left is to realise what we have planned. 

We have spend years into developing this vision and we will *not* compromise this vision by settling with less. 

Even if we fail or should give up at some point due to unforeseen reasons, people haven't to fear to loose anything, since in case that this should happen we will publish all of our work for everyone to use, to enable others to continue what we have started. The freely available, transparent documentation is also serving this goal. But as of now, we aren't inclined to give up and we keep working, as slowly as it may be.  

But we also do not want to keep people waiting forever. And so we can do something else. We can change our action plan:  
Instead of insisting on publishing everything or nothing, we can publish one thing at a time and give you something to play right away.  


## A new Action Plan 

We do not have to compromise our vision, because our project goal remains unaffected. But we can give you something of our work and can let you be part of it. We can let you experience at least a fracture of what we are working on. 

Starting from X [first release date] on, we will regularly publish a new build of the game. Through these builds we will slowly transition from Gothic Vanilla to Phoenix. 

By doing so, we will travel back in time. The first build will contain the Vanilla Story, but Alpha visuals and diverse fixes. It won't contain the Phoenix world you may know from our renderings and screenshots, but a version of the world as in version ~0.92, as well as the respective textures. 

Based on this simple baseline that you can actually play from start to finish, we will then add one additional feature at a time. 

Among the builds will be specific milestone builds, which represent one particular version of the world and story in the development history of Gothic and Phoenix. 

Slowly over time we will add additional features and story. Players can expect e.g. the "Old Camp Overhaul" as an update. There will be a New Camp Overhaul, a Psi Camp Overhaul. Our Attribute System and other mechanics, everything will slowly be added in, one build at a time, with the stated goal to keep all of these builds playable from start to finish. 

This way, you will have something to play, something to enjoy visually. We have more motivation and more regular perceptions of success. And maybe we will receive more help as well. May this new action plan nourish the Phoenix. Because we have enough of the ashes. It is about time to burn.
