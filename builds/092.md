# Phoenix Build 0.01 

In short, this build is what Gothic may have looked like...
* if the game world and visuals (armor textures etc.) wouldn't have been changed in the last few months of development, but would have been preserved as presented in the promotional material, last seen in ~v0.8. 
* if the cut content (levels, characters, dialogues and missions) from the time between v0.9 and v1.06 wouldn't have been removed (earlier cut content not yet included).
* if Mike had added the Vanilla story more or less as he has done it for v1.06, but without giving up on the original story; if they instead had been able to postpone the release further, preparing a development build in March 2001 with placeholders for all the lacking content to be added later instead of a finished product full of cuts and compromises. 
* if a majority of bugs would have been fixed. 

This means that the build will come with the following...

## Features
* Restored v0.94 Overworld, with the later addition of the Mountain Fortress from v1.00, including:
  * Old Pass & Northern Mountains
  * Alpha Monastery
  * Plains west from the Old Camp
  * HighWood in the Southwest
* Restored Underworld from ~0.9 to 1.00, including: 
  * Abandoned Mine (latest original version, largely incomplete, not yet included in the story)
  * OrcCity (latest original version, largely incomplete, not yet included in the story; one has to teleport behind the temple gate like in Vanilla, which serves as a placeholder and skip for all the story supposed to take place inbetween)
  * Sleeper Temple from v0.94-1.00, with the later addition of the Summoning Hall. 
* Restored Alpha Armor Textures by Bloody & Flosha
* Cut NPCs and Routines of v0.9[]
* Cut & fixed Missions contained in v1.00-1.06, including:
  * Diegos original (optional) ECTS tutorial 
  * Jesse's Protection Money 
  * Baal Namibs Drug Monopoly
  * Blackmailers
  * Roaming Bandits
* Mikes Release Version Story (v1.06) as it was created in a hurry within the last few months of development at the end of 2000 till 2001, but with placeholders for the intended additional story to be added later.
* The latest remaining Alpha Mechanics from 0.92 restored:
   * Additional Attributes: *Willpower* (+ *Psi Energy*), to restore the separate magic systems of Psionic Magic and Arcane Magic.
   * Inventory Limitation, excluding mission items (this feature will be completely unbalanced due to the other systems not yet being in place).
* Alpha Interface from ~v0.8 to 1.06:
  * 0.8 bars as the default HUD; 0.9 bars and Vanilla Bars selectable from the Menu
  * Alpha Fonts for Books and Notes restored.
  * Names of characters, items and interactible objects are displayed at the top of the screen by default, above the HP bar, as in all versions known until ~0.92.
* Auronen's Gothic Patch (the most complete compilation of fixes to date, contains many fixes that haven't been fixed anywhere before). 

---

What is not included:
* No Cutscenes. There are placeholders to signify scripted ingame events to be added later.
* No Alpha Mechanics yet other than the intended division of Psi Magic & Arcane Magic and the Limited Inventory.
* No additional changes to the world based on older Alpha Versions and no reconstructions of Concept Art.
* No additional Armours or Clothes as known from older versions or Concept Art. 

---

In short: Restores all cut content from the time between 0.92 and 1.06. Includes no older cut content, old story iterations or concept art reconstructions.
