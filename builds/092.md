# Phoenix Build 0.01 

In short, this build is what Gothic may have looked like...
* if the game world and visuals (armor textures etc.) wouldn't have been changed in the last few months of development, but would have been preserved as presented in the promotional material, last seen in ~v0.8. 
* if the cut content (levels, characters, dialogues and missions) from the time between v0.9 and v1.06 wouldn't have been removed (earlier cut content not yet included).
* if Mike had added the Vanilla story more or less as he has done it for v1.06, but without giving up on the original story; if they instead had been able to postpone the release further, preparing a development build in March 2001 with placeholders for all the lacking content to be added later instead of a finished product full of cuts and compromises. 
* if a majority of bugs would have been fixed. 

This means that the build will come with the following...

## Features
* Original v0.94 World Model with the Mountain Fortress of v1.00 added in, including:
  * Old Pass & Northern Mountains
  * Alpha Monastery
  * Plains west from the Old Camp
  * HighWood in the Southwest
  * Abandoned Mine (latest original version, not yet included in the story)
  * OrcCity (latest original version, largely incomplete and not yet included in the story; one has to teleport behind the gate like in Vanilla, which serves as a placeholder and skip for all the story supposed to take place inbetween) 
* Restored Alpha Armor Textures by Bloody & Flosha
* Cut NPCs and Routines of v0.9[]
* Mikes Release Version Story (v1.06) as it was created in a hurry within the last few months of development at the end of 2000 till 2001, but with placeholders for the intended additional story to be added later. 
* Auronen's Gothic Patch (the most complete compilation of fixes to date, contains many fixes that haven't been fixed anywhere before). 

0.92 World with the necessary later additions (Mountain Fortress) to fit to the Vanilla Story as it was created 

Restores all cut content from the time between 0.92 and 1.06. Includes no older cut content, old story iterations or concept art reconstructions.   

* **Overworld:** Alpha world version 0.92 (ECTS Demo), Alpha model and textures, with added Mountain Fortress.
  * PSI: Vanilla with added secret passage to Kaloms lab. 
  * OC: Vanilla with added Water pfx, blue roof textures etc. 
  * NC: Vanilla?
  * OM: Guarded as in screens.
  * North: Alpha Pass into Valley or not?
* **Underworld:** 0.92 Sleeper Temple (with added Summoning Hall) 
* **Characters:** 0.92 Character visuals (human faces, armors, clothes, monsters) and routines; Cut Characters included
* **Story:** Vanilla Story, with a few Alpha Missions, dialogues and scenes included: 
  * Jesse's Mission restored
  * Baal Namib's Drug Monopoly
  * Blackmailers included
  * Roaming Bandits included
  * Basic 0.94 Conviction. Maybe restore like in old Orpheus build. Cut intro before conviction.
  * Optional Diego tutorial as in 0.94.
* **Features:** 
  * Interface: (1) 0.92 bars as the default HUD of this version; 0.8 bars and Vanilla Bars selectable from the Menu. (2) Alpha Fonts for Books and Notes reconstructed. (3) Names of characters and items are displayed at the top of the screen by default.
  * Limited Inventory via slots, excluding mission items.   
* **Fixes:** Based on Auronens Gothic Patch (containing diverse fixes of the base game, the most ultimate patch to date)
