# Phoenix Build 0.01 

Build 0.01 is what Gothic may have looked like...
* if the game world and visuals (armor textures etc.) wouldn't have been changed in the last few months of development, but would have been preserved as presented in the promotional material, last seen in ~v0.8. 
* if the cut content (levels, characters, dialogues and missions) from the time between v0.9 and v1.06 wouldn't have been removed (earlier cut content not yet included).
* if Mike had added the Vanilla story more or less as he has done it for v1.06, but without giving up on the original story; if they instead had been able to postpone the release further, preparing a development build in March 2001 with placeholders for all the lacking content to be added later instead of a finished product full of cuts and compromises, to give an idea of what it could become. 
* if a majority of bugs would have been fixed. 

Based on these premises, the build comes with the following features... 

## Features
* Restored v0.94 Overworld, with the later addition of the Mountain Fortress from v1.00, including:
  * Old Pass & Northern Mountains
  * Alpha Monastery
  * Plains west from the Old Camp
  * HighWood in the Southwest
  * Guarded OM as on the Screenshots
  * Original Psi Temple, but with the addition of the Vanilla Temple due to the 1.06 version of the story (it will later be moved deeper into the Swamp and become part of additional story explorations on the Psi Path). 
* Restored Underworld from ~0.9 to 1.00, including: 
  * Abandoned Mine (latest original version, largely incomplete, not yet included in the story, thus regularly inaccessible)
  * OrcCity (latest original version, largely incomplete, not yet included in the story, but accessible; it serves the same purpose as the recycled Vanilla city – the player has to teleport behind the temple gate like in Vanilla, which serves as a mere placeholder and conventional skip for all the story supposed to take place inbetween)
  * Sleeper Temple from v0.94-1.00, with the later addition of the Summoning Hall. 
* Restored Alpha Armor Textures by Bloody & Flosha
* Cut NPCs and Routines of v0.9[]
* Cut Dialogues from v0.94 to v1.04 (mostly unvoiced)
* Cut & fixed Missions contained in v1.00-1.06, including:
  * Diegos original (optional) ECTS tutorial
  * All ECTS quests mentioned in the Demo Walkthrough
  * Jesse's Protection Money 
  * Baal Namibs Drug Monopoly
  * Blackmailers
  * Ambient Bandit Camps
* Mikes Release Version Story (v1.06) as it was created in a hurry within the last few months of development at the end of 2000 till 2001, but with placeholders for the intended additional story to be added later.
* The latest remaining Alpha Mechanics from 0.92 restored:
   * Additional Attributes: *Willpower* (+ *Psi Energy* as a necessary addition), to restore the separate magic systems of Psionic Magic and Arcane Magic.
   * Inventory Limitation, excluding mission items (this feature will be completely unbalanced in this build, due to the other systems not yet being in place).
* Alpha Interface from ~v0.8 to 1.06:
  * 0.8 bars as the default HUD; 0.9 bars and Vanilla Bars are optionally selectable.  
  * Unused Fonts (from 0.8 to 1.6) for Books and Notes restored.
  * Restored Original character sheet and diary textures.
  * ~v0.92 Main Menu & loading screen reconstructed.
  * Names of characters, items and interactible objects are displayed at the top of the screen by default, above the HP bar, as in all versions known until ~0.92.
* Auronen's Script Patch (the most complete compilation of script fixes to date, contains many fixes that haven't been fixed anywhere before). 

---

What is not or not yet included:
* No Content added after the original release v0.16 (this includes chapter screens and Chromanin). 
* No Cutscenes. There are placeholders to signify scripted ingame events to be added later.
* No Alpha Mechanics yet other than the intended division of magic and the Limited Inventory.
* No additional changes to the world based on older Alpha Versions and no reconstructions of Concept Art (Floshas updates to the world will be added in later builds).
* No additional Armours or Clothes as known from older versions or Concept Art.
* No single additional dialogue line or text by ourselves or by PB that isn't included in one of the builds between v0.94 and v1.06. 

---

In short: Restores all cut content from the time between 0.92 and 1.06. Includes no older cut content, older story iterations or concept art reconstructions.
